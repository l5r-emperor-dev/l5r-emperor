# Used printed abilities are marked on the card art (issue #1309)

**Status:** accepted (Mendon, 2026-11-22). Replaces the numbered USED/UNUSED strip proposed in #1278 for #1201.

**Decision.** Nothing is added outside a card to show which printed abilities are used: cards already carry two rails. Instead:

- A spent ability gets a 60%-transparent grey box over its printed paragraph on the scan. It clears when the ability can be used again (turn start, or any effect that restores a use).
- An ability usable more than once (Tsuruchi Tomaru, twice per turn) shows a small "1X", "2X"... in the card's clan colour once used; the grey box appears when the last use is spent.
- Once-per-game abilities stay grey for the rest of the game once used. Border Keep's first-turn ability greys when its owner's first turn ends, used or not.
- Abilities on attachments grey on the attachment itself.
- Tactician is the only keyword used up per card (each Tactician may perform one Tactical action per turn; Moto Taigo three). A printed Tactician gets the grey box over the keyword. A granted Tactician turns its grant marker red (the lacquer seal for a permanent grant, the "+ TACTICIAN" band for a temporary one), and hover reads "This personality has used tactical advantage this turn."
- Conquest (once per turn per player) and Naval Invasion (once per battle per player) are not marked.

**How.** The engine publishes uses and limits (`CardView.AbilityUses`, `CardView.TacticalUses`); the client only draws them. Boxes are rectangles on each scan in `src/L5R.Client/Content/ability-regions.json`, generated by `tools/content/ability_regions/build.py` (Windows OCR plus hand-checked `overrides.json`). Drawing at runtime costs one translucent quad per used ability; pre-rendered card variants were rejected (about 1,500 extra images, +200 MB, a 20–25 ms decode per swap).

**Open.** The grey box is subtle at table zoom by design; revisit opacity after play. Granted abilities that come from Strategies (no printed paragraph on the receiving card) keep their existing grant-panel display.
