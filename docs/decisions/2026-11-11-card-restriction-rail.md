# Card restriction display — issue #1200

Alfredo selected revised concept A in the 11 November 2026 chat and requested implementation with native crowded-unit, attachment and six-token validation in upright and bowed states.

Use two tracks in the existing physical rail: tokens and permanent grants on the inner track, active restrictions on the outer track. Upright units place rails to the left of the complete attachment stack. Bowed units place them below the complete stack. Each attachment keeps its own selectable rail band. Keep the full card face, upper/right Force standard and attack tab, selection outline, lower/right dishonor mark, and copy marker visible. Position the copy marker outside the combined rail footprint.

Restriction sentences and lifetimes come from the engine's public view. The client displays them in inspection and hover and never infers action legality. Preserve the old action-lock marker for older views without the optional list. Source identity is nullable: concealed or historically unrecorded sources must not be invented or disclosed. Adding this presentation does not alter what any effect does.

Artwork uses the approved raster seal derivative; see `src/L5R.Client/Art/Restrictions/PROVENANCE.json`. Other restriction categories reuse the established action-lock seal. Initial coverage targets the issue's priority stored movement/assignment, straighten, action, targeting and protection systems; this decision is not a claim that every catalogue restriction or static immunity has been projected.
