# Conan strategy campaign, step 4: the lookahead's place (issue #1206, from 2026-11-15)

Branch `claude/1206-conan-step4`, from `main` after the Calibration refit (PR #1247). Mendon's order of work (2026-11-14): "continue on the lettered families then refit the calibration tables. Then move on to step 4."

## The question (CONAN_STRATEGY_BRIEF.md step 4)

Measure, on one fresh block against T2, with thinking times: (1) the rules alone; (2) the rules plus today's lookahead; (3) the rules plus a lookahead that takes the Read's shapes as candidates (the lone unit, the split, the Cavalry fork) and its exchange as the playout's battle policy. Done when the three are in one table and the arrangement with the highest win rate, or the fastest within noise, is chosen. A rules-only Conan that matches the others is the better product: it answers at once.

## What "the rules" are after step 3

Step 3 promoted nothing to a default: families D, A, E, F and G were shelved null or negative; C (`readC`, +28 at Contender speed, null under Hard) and B (`readB`, `rbCav`, +15 and +14, null under Hard) are opt-in; I's hand formulation (`readI`, `riHand`, +11) is in its sequential test. So "the rules alone" is T2 itself, and the question becomes whether the search earns its thinking time, and whether the Read improves the search's candidates and playouts where it could not improve the rules.

## Arms (pre-registered)

| Arm | Rules | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| `t2` | `T2Rules` | the rules alone (the A/A of the block doubles as its baseline) |
| `hard` | T2 + `lkDef, lkVar, lkPlan` + `look` | today's lookahead (Conan-best) |
| `hardRead` | Hard + `read, lkRead` | the Read's attack shapes (the opportunity's options: lone unit, split, sufficient, everything) added to `lkPlan`'s candidates |
| `hardFight` | Hard + `read, lkFight` | the playout bots play their battles by the Fight (family E's exchange policy) instead of the Contender's battle rules |
| `hardBoth` | Hard + `read, lkRead, lkFight` | both |

All against T2 on block 11700 (4 seeds, 1,456 games), 10-minute budget, Conan arms run together under identical load (`lkT` is wall-clock); thinking times from `tools/bots/think_times.py`. The A/A `ca117` gives the paired baseline for `t2`; the Conan arms are compared with each other by paired games (`compare_tournament.py --baseline hard --arm ...`).

## Flags added

- `lkRead` (search): `AttackShapes` also yields the Read's opportunity options as candidate assignments, labelled by the option (`read:lone`, `read:split`, ...), de-duplicated against the existing shapes.
- `lkFight` (search): the playout bots of the viewer's seat carry `read, readE` (the Fight decides inside battles in playouts; the real decision is still the search's).

## Arena protocol

Hard's lookahead is bounded by wall-clock time (`lkT`), so Conan arms are compared only when run together under identical load (family C's note). The Mac runs three Conan arms at once at most, so the step 4 arms run in batches of two on the same block: `hard` beside `hardRead`, `hard` beside `hardFight`, `hard` beside `hardBoth`, each batch with its own `hard` run as the paired baseline (`compare_tournament.py --baseline hard<batch> --arm ...`), 10-minute budget, no fire counting. The `t2` arm is the block's A/A (`ca117`). Thinking times come from `tools/bots/think_times.py` on each arm's logs. 1,456 games an arm (four seeds, 11700-11703); if the Mac's time is short, two seeds (728) with the result stated as such.

## lkRead on the decision set

`L5R_POS_ARMS="hr:+look,lkDef,lkVar,lkPlan,lkB=600,lkT=60,read,lkRead" L5R_POS_FAMILY=A L5R_POS_LOG=1`: at Mendon's five split positions the search compares the Contender's plan and eight shapes (A-G3T9: `contender 16.1 | shape 2 4.1 | shape 3 4.1 | shape 1 5.3 | ... | shape 3 18.5 -> 6`, 4.5 s, 389 playout decisions) where the Contender's own shapes are four (all at the weakest, enough to raze, spread, probe): the Read's options are in the candidate set. Agreement at family A's positions is 2 of 9, as `conan` without lkRead; the search still picks by playing on.

## Result (block 11700, 4 seeds, against T2, 10-minute budget; run 2026-11-15/16)

| Arm | Win v T2 | Paired against its own Hard run | Thinking per game (mean) | Per search median / p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `t2` (the rules alone; A/A `ca117`) | **50.5%** (735 of 1,456) | — | none | — |
| `hard` (today's lookahead), three runs | **71.6%, 71.8%, 70.8%** (mean 71.4%, 3,118 of 4,366) | — | 77 to 84 s | 2.0–2.2 s / 6.2–6.9 s |
| `hardRead` (+ the Read's shapes) | 71.0% (1,033 of 1,454) | 45 gained, 53 lost, **net −8**, p .48 | 89 s | 2.1 s / 9.0 s |
| `hardFight` (+ the Fight in playouts) | 70.4% (1,025 of 1,456) | 81 gained, 102 lost, **net −21**, p .14 | 81 s | 2.1 s / 6.7 s |
| `hardBoth` (both) | 68.8% (1,002 of 1,455) | 87 gained, 116 lost, **net −29**, p .049 | 93 s | 2.1 s / 9.7 s |

**Choice: today's lookahead (`hard`), unchanged.** The search is worth about 21 points over the rules alone (71.4% against 50.5%), so a rules-only Conan is not a product at this level. Neither of the Read's contributions improves the search: its shapes add candidates the playouts do not prefer and slow the slow searches (p90 6.2 s → 9.0 s), and the Fight inside the playouts plays the battles worse than the Contender's battle rules do, as it did at the table (family E, −49 and −27). Together they cost 29 games in 1,455 (two-sided p .049). `lkRead` and `lkFight` stay as opt-in flags; nothing changes on main's defaults.

What this means for stage 1: after steps 3 and 4 the best measured Conan is today's Hard, at 71.4% against T2 on block 11700 (72.7 to 74.5% on the three earlier blocks), short of the 80% bar. No step 3 rule and no step 4 arrangement moved it under the lookahead. Step 5 (confirmation) would measure the same bot as main's Hard.
