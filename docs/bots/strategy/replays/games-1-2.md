# Human decision model: games 1 and 2

Sources: `practice-20261109-133956.compare.log` (G1, Unicorn Cavalry v Lion, Honor win T12) and `practice-20261109-141352.compare.log` (G2, Crane v Spider, Honor win T17).

Abbreviations: F = Force; PS = Province Strength; H a/b = his Honor / opponent's; G = Gold; "hand n" = opponent's hand size (public); "x v y" = attacking Force v defending Force (unbowed cards only). Bot: `=` agreed, `≠` differed.

Rules that drive most of what follows (checked in COMPREHENSIVE_RULES.md): the higher army Force destroys the whole opposing army, bowed cards included; a tie destroys both; the winner gains 2 Honor per card destroyed; PS only decides whether the Province falls; Battle actions need a unit of yours at the battlefield (Rule of Presence); the first clan Personality bought at full price can be Proclaimed for Honor equal to Personal Honor (PH), others can take a 2 Gold discount instead.

**Headline.** Across both games he attacked three times (G1 T6, G2 T3, G2 T5), destroyed one Province and lost one army. He won both games on Honor from three sources: a Proclaim almost every turn, killing attackers on defence (12 + 16 Honor), and small repeatable Honor triggers. The hypothesis about defence is strongly supported. The hypothesis about Cavalry forcing easy Province kills is barely tested: one Cavalry attack in two games.

## 5. Candidate rules

1. **Defence assignment (strong: G1 T5, T9; G2 T10, T12, T16).** IF every attacker is at one Province AND the opponent has no unassigned Cavalry THEN assign every unbowed unit with 2F or more there. ELSE at each attacked Province assign Force above the attackers' where possible; where not, assign one unit able to perform the control cards in hand, and count one card per attacker. Always keep 0–1F Personalities with Gold or Honor abilities home.
2. **Battle tempo (strong: G1 T5, T9; G2 T10, T12, T16).** IF my unbowed Force exceeds theirs THEN pass. IF an opposing action makes Force level or worse THEN play the smallest effect that restores a lead. IF the opponent passes while I lead THEN pass and keep the rest. Exceptions: G1 T5, where he led with his weakest disruptive card while only 2F ahead of an opponent holding six cards; G2 T16 P2, where he passed at 9 v 3 and would have lost the unit had Spider passed too.
3. **Kill rather than repel (strong: G1 T9; G2 T10, T12, T16).** IF I can end ahead on Force THEN use bows and Force cuts, which leave the attacker in the battle to die for 2 Honor per card, AND hold send-home effects as the fallback. ELSE IF I cannot win on Force (G2 T8, only defender bowed) THEN send attackers home to save my unit.
4. **Favor on defence (strong: G2 T8, T10, T12, T16; G1 T9).** IF level or behind on Force at my first opportunity THEN spend the Favor before any card. IF ahead THEN keep it. Target the largest attacker when behind (T12, T16); T10 contradicts (he sent the smaller home and killed the larger).
5. **Lobbying (strong: G1 T8; G2 T7, T9, T11, T13, T15).** IF I do not hold the Favor AND my Honor is higher THEN lobby every turn with the Personality whose bowing costs least: 0F, or one another ability can straighten. Not done on G2 T3 or T5, when the only Personality was needed to attack.
6. **Favor to Honor (single: G2 T15; T13 declined).** IF the Favor went unused through the opponent's turn AND I can lobby again this turn THEN convert it once. Never end my turn without it.
7. **Free repeatable disruption (strong: G2 T8–T16).** Use a no-card "bow or I gain Honor" ability every turn, both players' turns, performed by the lowest-Force eligible Personality, and straighten that performer with any spare ability. On the opponent's turn wait for their first Action and target the unit they invested in, unless bowing no longer removes its Force (T6, T14; T16 contradicts).
8. **Attack declaration (strong for declining: G1 T8; G2 T7–T15. Moderate for attacking: G1 T6; G2 T3, T5).** IF the Force I send exceeds the unbowed defenders I cannot neutralise plus PS AND I hold a neutraliser for each likely defender AND the opponent's possible counter-attack cannot exceed what I leave home plus PS THEN attack. ELSE do not.
9. **Attack window (moderate: G1 T6; G2 T5).** Prefer the turn on which the opponent's strongest units are bowed, from their own attack or from paying a cost.
10. **Commitment size (single: G1 T6).** IF the opponent visibly holds a send-home effect I cannot stop THEN attack with one self-sufficient unit and keep the rest unbowed at home.
11. **Cavalry timing (single: G1 T6).** Pass the infantry step, see the defence, then assign Cavalry only where Force exceeds defenders plus PS.
12. **Province choice (moderate: G1 T6; G2 T5).** Attack the Province holding the opponent's most dangerous face-up Personality. Inferred from the two choices; the log gives no reason.
13. **Honor lock (moderate: G1 T10; G2 T15).** IF Honor plus this turn's certain gains reaches 40 AND the opponent cannot plausibly break my defence THEN do not attack, keep every unit unbowed, spend Gold on Honor effects first and Proclaimed bodies second, and ignore Holdings.
14. **Purchase order (strong: G1 T4, T6, T10; G2 T5, T7, T9, T11, T13, T15).** Buy a Gold-producing Personality first and use its Gold. Pay full price and Proclaim the highest-PH Personality; take the discount on the rest. Buy Holdings whose cost falls per Personality last. Exception (G2 T1): take the discount when 2 Gold decides a second purchase.
15. **Economy v bodies (moderate each branch).** IF the opponent has no Force able to exceed my PS next turn AND one Personality would take the whole turn's Gold THEN buy Holdings and refresh Personalities away (G1 T2). ELSE IF I have no Force-bearing unit THEN buy bodies first and Holdings with the remainder (G1 T4; G2 T5). ELSE IF Gold covers the row THEN buy all of it (G2 T7–T13). ELSE one Personality plus one Holding (G1 T6, T8).
16. **Early pressure (single: G2 T1–T3).** IF my hand can turn a cheap Personality into a unit above PS AND the opponent has no Personalities THEN buy it on turn 1, equip, and attack at once.
17. **Attachments (moderate: G1 T6, T8, T10; G2 T9).** Put Force and Honor attachments on the unit that will fight, and only with Gold not needed for a Personality this turn. Exception: G2 T3, when the opponent had no bodies.
18. **Hold cards with no present effect (strong: G1 T5, T8, T9; G2 T9, T11, T14).** Do not play a one-shot card for a marginal gain: a Force boost when not attacking, a straighten for 1 Honor, a bow before an attack I can beat.
19. **Aiming Force cuts (moderate: G1 T5, T9).** Aim a flexible cut at the highest-Force Follower and never stack cuts beyond a card's Force; keep enemy Personalities for effects that can only target Personalities.

## 6. Where the bot differed and it mattered

- **G2 T12 defence (0F unit alone v 24F).** With the Favor and one send-home card, at least 9F would remain against PS 6: the Province falls. He held it, killed three, gained 6 Honor.
- **G2 T16 defence (all units at one Province).** Two Provinces attacked by 9F each would have been undefended and destroyed. He held all three and killed four.
- **G1 T9 Favor at the first opportunity, and one defender.** Sending the 8F unit home forfeits its three kills (6 Honor) and the Favor; a lone 4F defender dies to the remaining 10F. His line gained 12 Honor and killed six cards.
- **G1 T6 both Maidens committed.** After Lion's Favor removed Ryoko, 3F v 3F is a tie: the second Maiden dies.
- **G1 T8 declare attack.** 12F into 15F of unbowed defenders plus a −3F ability; likely loss of units for nothing.
- **G1 T4 and T6 Holding before Personality.** One body instead of two before Lion's T5 attack; on T6 the second Ryoko becomes unaffordable.
- **G2 T1 refresh the cheap Personality away.** No T3 attack, so no Province destroyed (the only one in either game).
- **G2 T10 send-home instead of Force cuts.** Holds the Province but forfeits the kill; his line risked three Personalities on the exchange and won it.
- **G2 T13 and T15 repeated Favor-to-Honor.** He would have had no Favor for the T16 defence, where it removed the 8F attacker.
- **G1 T10 destroy own Province for 4 Honor.** Unnecessary; he reached 45 without it.
- **G1 T5 bow card before the attack.** Prevents the attack; Lion keeps the four Battle cards it burned.
