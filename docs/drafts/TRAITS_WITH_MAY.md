# Traits containing the word "may"

Source: the Emperor card catalogue (`content/oracle/emperor-catalogue.json`), current Oracle text. A trait here is any line of card text with no action or reaction colon (reminder text in parentheses and quoted granted abilities ignored). Includes "may not" prohibitions; those are flagged.

**122 traits on 120 cards.**

## Celestial (5)

- **Akodo's Guidance - exp**: Lion Clan players may target defending enemy Personalities with the rulebook Favor Political Battle action.
- **Blessings of the Shi-Tien Yen-Wang**: After your turn begins, you may choose your dead Personality and put him at the top of your deck.
- **Doji's Guidance - exp**: Crane Clan players may take the rulebook Favor Political Battle action even if they control no units at the current battlefield.
- **Hantei's Guidance**: The rulebook's Favor Political Battle action has Absent for you, and you may target any Personality with it. (Absent actions can be taken without presence.)
- **Saibankan's Justice** *(may not)*: Other players may not lobby.
